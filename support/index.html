Support

Need a hand with StoryParts?

Email cazallasquared@gmail.com. Include your app version, iOS version and the steps that caused the problem. Do not send private videos or sensitive information unless you want us to review them.

How do I split and share a video?

Import from Photos or Files, choose the part duration, preview the clips and save or share them. For Instagram or Facebook, save to Photos and select the clips in order in the destination app. For WhatsApp, choose an available destination in the iOS share sheet. Confirm publication in StoryParts after returning.

Why does the destination change the order or duration?

The destination app controls its own limits, order and compression. Check its preview. Use shorter parts or share one clip at a time if needed.

Why is saving unavailable?

Check available device storage and StoryParts photo permissions in iOS Settings. Videos stored in iCloud may need to download before processing.

How do I restore an ad-free purchase?

If an ad-free purchase is available in your version, use Restore Purchases in StoryParts settings with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Purchase availability can vary by build. Core video tools remain available without a purchase.

How do I delete a story?

Delete it from the app’s library. Copies you saved to Photos, Files or another app must be deleted there separately. Removing StoryParts also removes its local app data; backups are controlled by your Apple settings.

Soporte en español

Escríbenos a cazallasquared@gmail.com indicando la versión de la app, la versión de iOS y los pasos del problema.

Importa desde Fotos o Archivos, elige la duración, revisa las partes y guarda o comparte. Para Instagram y Facebook, guarda en Fotos y selecciona los clips en orden desde la app de destino. Para WhatsApp, utiliza las opciones disponibles en la hoja de compartir.

El destino controla los límites, la compresión y el orden final. Si cambia el resultado, prueba con partes más cortas o comparte una a una. StoryParts no publica automáticamente ni verifica las publicaciones.

Para borrar una historia, elimínala de la biblioteca. Las copias exportadas se borran por separado desde Fotos, Archivos o la app donde las guardaste.
