Need a hand with StoryParts?

Email cazallasquared@gmail.com. Include your app version, iOS version and the steps that caused the problem. Do not send private videos or sensitive information unless you want us to review them.

How do I split and share a video?

Import from Photos or Files and choose 30, 60 or 90 seconds per part. Preview the whole story with audio before creating the clips. Choose Create & share to open sharing straight away, or Only create the parts to save the story in your library. For Instagram or Facebook, save to Photos and select the clips in order in the destination app. For WhatsApp, choose an available destination in the iOS share sheet. You can share the whole story or switch to individual parts. StoryParts remembers the selected part, but does not post automatically or confirm publication.

Why does the destination change the order or duration?

The destination app controls its own limits, order and compression. Check its preview. Use shorter parts or share one clip at a time if needed.

Why is saving unavailable?

Check available device storage and StoryParts photo permissions in iOS Settings. Videos stored in iCloud may need to download before processing. If you deleted an exported copy, open the story and choose Save to Photos again. Saving again creates new copies, so the app asks before doing so.

How do I restore an ad-free purchase?

Use Restore Purchases in StoryParts settings with the Apple Account used for the purchase. The optional one-time purchase removes ads without a subscription. Core video tools remain available without a purchase.

How do I delete a story?

Delete it from the app’s library. Copies you saved to Photos, Files or another app must be deleted there separately. Removing StoryParts also removes its local app data; backups are controlled by your Apple settings.

Soporte en español

Escríbenos a cazallasquared@gmail.com indicando la versión de la app, la versión de iOS y los pasos del problema.

Importa desde Fotos o Archivos, elige partes de 30, 60 o 90 segundos y revisa la historia completa con audio. Crear y compartir abre directamente las opciones para compartir; Solo crear las partes deja la historia en tu biblioteca. Puedes compartir toda la historia o una parte cada vez. La app recuerda la parte seleccionada. Para Instagram y Facebook, guarda en Fotos y selecciona los clips en orden desde la app de destino. Para WhatsApp, utiliza las opciones disponibles en la hoja de compartir.

El destino controla los límites, la compresión y el orden final. Si cambia el resultado, prueba con partes más cortas o comparte una a una. StoryParts no publica automáticamente ni verifica las publicaciones.

Si borraste los clips de Fotos, abre la historia y elige Volver a guardar en Fotos. La app avisa antes de crear nuevas copias. Para restaurar la compra única sin anuncios, usa Restaurar compras en Ajustes con la misma cuenta de Apple. Para borrar una historia, elimínala de la biblioteca. Las copias exportadas se borran por separado desde Fotos, Archivos o la app donde las guardaste.
